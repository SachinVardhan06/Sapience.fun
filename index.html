FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Find a starting point, understand what an account saves, and know what to expect from the current site.

What is Sapience?

Sapience helps you learn cybersecurity through short, interactive lessons and practical website defense checks. Explore authentication, common web attacks, software safety, and incident response, then follow public research links to see how security issues are investigated.

Where should I start if I am new to cybersecurity?

Start with the first lesson in Learn. Work through each step, try the defender quiz, then use the website checklist to connect the ideas to real protective actions. You can return to any lesson whenever you need a refresher.

Do I need an account?

No. The published lessons, quiz, checklist, and research archive are open to browse. A free account saves your completed lessons, checklist progress, bookmarks, private notes, and best quiz score so you can pick up where you left off.

What does the website checklist cover?

It contains 15 checks across access control, software updates, application safety, monitoring, and recovery. Use it as a learning and review aid; a checked item alone does not verify that a website is secure.

Where do the research links come from?

The research archive points to public write-ups by their original authors. Sapience helps you discover and filter them; open a link to read the full work at its source.

Is Sapience free?

Yes. Everything currently published is free to explore. The ₹199 monthly, ₹499 quarterly, and ₹1,599 yearly cards let you ask about membership; we confirm what a plan includes and how it is activated before any payment.

Is the 30-day trial available now?

You can explore all published lessons and tools now with a free account. For a 30-day membership trial, contact us to ask about availability and terms. No card is needed to create the free account.

Is new material being added?

The timeline shows target milestones from April through July 2027, including guided labs and broader learning paths. These are plans, so the dates and scope may change.

How can I share feedback or ask a question?

Sign in and use the feedback form above to tell us what helped or needs work. Feedback appears publicly only after review, using the display name you choose. For a direct question, send an email.
