Here is what this website uses to run your account and save your learning progress.

What we collect

If you create an account, Sapience stores your name, email address, and a salted password hash. It stores lesson and checklist progress, bookmarks, private notes, and your best quiz score when you are signed in. If you submit feedback, we store the display name, rating, message, and review status you provide.

If you browse without signing in, lesson, checklist, and study progress can be saved in this browser’s local storage. The site uses a session cookie when you sign in; it expires after 30 days. The server may process basic request information needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

We use account information to sign you in and show your saved work across visits. We use submitted feedback to review and improve the learning experience. Feedback appears publicly only after approval, using the display name and message you agreed to share.

Storage and security

Account and study data are kept in the service database. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, not plain text. Session cookies are HTTP-only and are marked Secure on the production HTTPS site. These measures help protect data, but no online service can promise absolute security.

We keep account and study data while the account remains active or until we process a deletion request. A copy may remain in operational backups until those backups are removed.

Outside services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Research links lead to independent websites with their own privacy practices. Membership inquiries open your email app and send information you choose to include to our contact address. This website does not collect payment card details.

Your choices

You can use the published lessons and tools without an account. You can clear saved local progress through your browser settings. To ask for a copy of your account data, correct it, or request account deletion, email vardhansachin@sapience.fun from the address on your account. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Questions about this page can go to the same address.
